# The five design rules (mandatory, checked in review)

1. **Clear goal and a loss condition.** The player always knows what to do and how they can lose. Both are visible on screen (HUD text, health bar, timer) within the first seconds.
2. **Game juice.** Every important action gives feedback: camera shake on impact, particle bursts, a sound effect, and tweens (squash/stretch, flashes, pop-ups). List the concrete effect for each key action.
3. **Risk/reward dilemma.** The player repeatedly chooses between a safe option and a greedier, more dangerous one that pays more. Describe the choice and its payoff.
4. **Difficulty progression.** Intensity rises across levels (new element, higher speed or density) with occasional short breathers. Easy/Normal/Hard presets differ in measurable values.
5. **Instant retry.** After a loss the player is back in action in under about 2 seconds, with no loading screen and no menu in between. State is reset in place.
